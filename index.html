Our intent

We would rather print forty things worth saying than four hundred that aren't.

Everything here started as a line someone was too polite to finish. We print it in full, on heavyweight cotton, and then we stop printing it.

No seasonal churn. No hundred-colourway drops. A short list, made properly, in black and white and one shade of red — because the joke should be the loudest thing you're wearing.
