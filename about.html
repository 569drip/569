V.VI.IX — five, six, nine — is a label for people who were never very good at keeping it to themselves.

We print the part everyone else edits out.

Most clothing hedges. It gestures at a personality without committing to one. We started V.VI.IX because the funniest, sharpest, most honest thing in any group chat is never the thing that makes it onto a shirt.

So we make the shirt. Text-based prints, drawn from dark humour and sex positivity, set in black and white and one shade of red so that nothing competes with what it actually says. There is a second thing coming, and we are not describing it yet.

Everything is made in short runs. When a run sells out we move on, because a joke repeated is a joke ruined. There is no seasonal calendar, no core range, and no plan to make forty colourways of anything.

We are a small operation. That is the point.

What we hold to

Four rules

01

Consent is the whole kink

Sex positivity without consent is just noise. It shows up in the designs because it shows up in how we operate.

02

Short runs, no reprints

Limited run, then it's retired. You will not see your shirt on six people at the same party.

03

Discreet by default

Plain packaging, neutral sender name, and anything you send us deleted once it has served its purpose. Nobody needs to know but you.

04

Funny, not cruel

The joke is never at somebody else's expense. If a design only works by punching down, it doesn't get printed.

Practical

Shipping,
returns,
and the rest

How long does an order take?

Stocked pieces are made to order and dispatched in 2–5 business days. Anything from a future drop will carry its own timings when it lands.

Where do you ship?

Worldwide, printed at the facility closest to you. Free over $80. Duties for outside your region are calculated at checkout, never as a surprise later.

What does the packaging look like?

Plain and unbranded, with a neutral sender name on the label. Nothing on the outside indicates what is inside or who it came from.

Can I return something?

Unworn pieces within 30 days, no explanation required. Made-to-order pieces that are personal to you will have their own terms, stated clearly before you buy.

What's the "custom art" thing you keep hinting at?

Not answering that yet. It's coming, it won't be something you pick off a grid, and no two will be the same.
